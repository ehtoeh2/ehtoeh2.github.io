Beyond the Lab

The person behind the research

Born in 1996 and raised in Incheon, I am the youngest of three siblings. This page brings together a few experiences and activities that have shaped who I am outside research.

A Personal Introduction

The places that shaped my early years

I was born in Gwangmyeong but spent most of my childhood in Incheon, which I consider my hometown. I later completed my military service in Cheorwon, where winter field training became one of the most demanding and memorable experiences of my early adulthood. I subsequently majored in physical education, and an interest in human thermoregulation became the first step in my research journey.

Outside Research

How I spend time beyond the lab

Football

A lifelong connection to football

I enjoy both playing and watching football. As a child, I once imagined pursuing the sport competitively, and during university I committed myself fully to my club team. We won several competitions together, including the tournament pictured here.

Weight Training

A routine built around steady progress

People often assume that I train every day, but I simply enjoy strength training as a regular part of my routine rather than an obsession. My current three-lift total is around 370 kg, which I treat as a personal benchmark for gradual progress.

Travel

New places, wider perspectives

I enjoy traveling and experiencing places that I had previously known only through photographs. The Grand Canyon trip pictured here remains one of my most memorable opportunities to step outside a familiar routine and see a new landscape firsthand.
