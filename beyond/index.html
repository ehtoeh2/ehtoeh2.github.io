Beyond the Lab

The person behind the research

Born in 1996 and raised in Incheon, I am the youngest of three siblings. This page brings together a few experiences and activities that have shaped who I am outside research.

A Personal Introduction

The places that shaped
my early years

I was born in Gwangmyeong but spent most of my childhood in Incheon, which I consider my hometown. I later completed my military service in Cheorwon, where winter field training became one of the most demanding and memorable experiences of my early adulthood. During my undergraduate training in physical education, I joined a short-term exchange program at San Diego State University in 2019, where I visited research laboratories and attended a range of seminars. Exposure to this new academic and research environment sparked my interest in pursuing opportunities abroad.

Outside Research

Football

A lifelong connection to football

I enjoy both playing and watching football. As a child, I imagined pursuing the sport competitively, and at university I committed myself fully to my club team. Over three years, we competed in five tournaments, winning three championships, finishing as runners-up once, and placing third once. In 2019, I had the opportunity to serve as team captain, leading the team both on and off the pitch. One of those championship moments is pictured here.

Weight Training & Sports

Strength, Variety, and Steady Progress

Weight training and sports are an important part of my daily life. I have maintained a consistent weight-training practice for more than eight years, while also enjoying a variety of seasonal activities, from water sports to skiing. For me, exercise is more than a way to stay fit; it is a continuing process of growth that encourages me to embrace new experiences and challenge myself to learn new ways of moving.

Travel

New Places, Wider Perspectives

I have enjoyed traveling since childhood, exploring unfamiliar places and experiencing different cultures firsthand. To date, I have had the opportunity to visit more than ten countries.

One of my most memorable experiences was backpacking across Europe with a friend for six weeks when I was twenty. It was my first extended trip abroad without my family, and we encountered many unexpected challenges along the way. Navigating them taught me to adapt and solve problems independently. The journey also gave me opportunities to connect with people from different backgrounds and encounter cultures and ways of life that were entirely new to me.

Together, these experiences taught me to embrace unfamiliar environments, learn through exploration, and see the world from a broader perspective.
