Beyond the Lab

The person behind the research

Born in 1996 and raised in Incheon, I am the youngest of three siblings. This page brings together a few experiences and activities that have shaped who I am outside research.

A Personal Introduction

The places that shaped my early years

I was born in Gwangmyeong but spent most of my childhood in Incheon, which I consider my hometown. I later completed my military service in Cheorwon, where winter field training became one of the most demanding and memorable experiences of my early adulthood. I subsequently majored in physical education, and an interest in human thermoregulation became the first step in my research journey.

How I spend time beyond the lab

A lifelong connection to football

Weight Training & Sports

Strength, variety, and steady progress

I enjoy weight training as a regular part of my routine, with a current three-lift total of around 370 kg. I also enjoy trying different sports, including bodyboarding and skiing. For me, staying active is about steady progress, variety, and the challenge of learning new movements.

I enjoy traveling and experiencing places that I had previously known only through photographs. From childhood trips to later journeys through the Alps and New York City, travel has given me opportunities to step outside familiar routines and experience new landscapes and cultures firsthand.
