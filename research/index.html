Research

Skeletal muscle biology across aging, disease, and adaptation

I investigate how skeletal muscle changes across physiological stress, disease, drug exposure, and aging, with an emphasis on translating complex data into biological mechanisms.

01 · Research Vision

Muscle Stem Cell Aging and Niche Remodeling

I aim to understand how aging alters muscle stem cells and their surrounding niche, and how exercise may preserve regenerative capacity. My approach will combine multi-omics analysis with targeted in vitro and in vivo validation.

Question 01

How does aging disrupt muscle stem cell-niche interactions?

Define how age-associated changes across neighboring cell populations, extracellular matrix, and inflammatory signals alter muscle stem cell quiescence, activation, and self-renewal.

Question 02

Can exercise remodel the aged muscle stem cell niche?

Determine whether exercise-induced changes in the local environment preserve muscle stem cell function and improve muscle regeneration during aging.

Question 03

Does cell-cycle entry restore DNA repair capacity?

Explore whether exercise-driven activation enables aged muscle stem cells to access high-fidelity DNA repair pathways and reduce accumulated genomic damage.

02 · Korea University & KIST

Skeletal Muscle Remodeling: From Molecular Signals to Tissue Models

My work at Korea University and KIST has centered on skeletal muscle biology, particularly muscle wasting, biomarker discovery, and drug-induced remodeling of the muscle microenvironment. In parallel, I contributed to 3D muscle-on-chip and brain organoid model development, broadening my understanding of tissue organization and cell-microenvironment interactions.

Project 01 · Skeletal Muscle

Cancer Cachexia Biomarker Discovery

Integrated bulk and single-nucleus RNA-seq with LC-MS/MS proteomics, mouse validation, and public human serum proteomics to identify clinically relevant, muscle-associated circulating biomarker candidates.

Project 02 · Muscle Microenvironment

Drug-Induced Remodeling of the Skeletal Muscle Microenvironment

Investigated how pharmacological treatment reshapes the skeletal muscle microenvironment and induces muscle atrophy, using human muscle biopsy samples, transcriptomic analysis, and experimental validation.

Project 03 · 3D Skeletal Muscle

3D Skeletal Muscle-on-Chip System

Contributed to the development of a 3D skeletal muscle-on-chip platform for modeling tissue organization and testing muscle responses within a controlled microenvironment.

Project 04 · Complementary Model

Establishment of Diverse Organoid Models

Contributed to the development of diverse organoid models, gaining hands-on experience in iPSC and patient-derived stem cell culture, 3D tissue organization, and organoid-based experimental systems.

03 · Kyung Hee University

Human Responses to Exercise and Environmental Stress

At the Environmental Exercise Physiology Lab, I led and contributed to controlled human studies examining cardiovascular, respiratory, thermoregulatory, and perceptual responses to physiological stress. This work resulted in six journal articles, including first- and co-first-author papers.

Study 01

Ischemic Preconditioning and Ambulatory Blood Pressure

Examined how ischemic preconditioning combined with aerobic exercise influences 24-hour blood pressure in men with prehypertension or stage 1 hypertension.

Study 02

Exercise and Seasonal Cold Adaptation

Investigated whether regular exercise alters thermoregulatory adaptation during winter cold exposure.

Study 03

Remote Ischemic Preconditioning and Reperfusion Injury

Evaluated whether a single bout of remote ischemic preconditioning suppresses ischemia-reperfusion injury in obese young men.

Study 04

Breathing Resistance During Exercise

Studied how added respiratory resistance affects pulmonary ventilation, exercise-induced hypoxemia, and aerobic performance.

Study 05

Thermal Sensitivity at Rest and During Exercise

Assessed how local cold and heat stimuli alter cutaneous thermal sensitivity and the inter-threshold zone at rest and during exercise.

Study 06

Respirator Fit and Wearing Practice

Compared filtering facepiece respirator fit across different mask shapes and wearing practices.
