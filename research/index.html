Research

Skeletal muscle biology across aging, disease, and adaptation

I investigate how skeletal muscle changes across physiological stress, disease, drug exposure, and aging, with an emphasis on translating complex data into biological mechanisms.

01 · Korea University & KIST

Skeletal Muscle Remodeling: From Molecular Signals to Tissue Models

My work at Korea University and KIST has centered on skeletal muscle biology, particularly muscle wasting, biomarker discovery, and drug-induced remodeling of the muscle microenvironment. In parallel, I contributed to 3D muscle-on-chip and brain organoid model development, broadening my understanding of tissue organization and cell-microenvironment interactions.

Project 01 · Skeletal Muscle

Cancer Cachexia Biomarker Discovery

I led this study as the first author. To address the lack of objective markers for early cancer cachexia detection, we developed an integrated multi-omics and sensor-based framework linking skeletal muscle remodeling to circulating signals. We combined bulk and single-nucleus RNA-seq with proteomics to identify secreted, muscle-associated biomarker candidates, validated their elevation in muscle and circulation across sexes, and confirmed their clinical relevance through re-analysis of human skeletal muscle transcriptomes. We then developed a highly sensitive field-effect transistor biosensor that detected subtle serum changes before marked muscle strength decline, providing a framework for early detection and longitudinal monitoring of cancer cachexia.

Working manuscript

An integrated multi-omics and sensor framework identifies early detectable circulating biomarkers in cancer cachexia

First author 2026

Project 02 · Muscle Microenvironment

Drug-Induced Remodeling of the Skeletal Muscle Microenvironment

I participated in this study as a co-author. We investigated how pharmacological treatment reshapes the skeletal muscle microenvironment and induces muscle atrophy using human muscle biopsy samples, transcriptomic analysis, and experimental validation.

Project status Ongoing research

Project 03 · 3D Skeletal Muscle

3D Skeletal Muscle-on-Chip System

I contributed to the development of a 3D skeletal muscle-on-chip platform for modeling tissue formation and organization and evaluating skeletal muscle responses within a controlled microenvironment. Through this work, I gained experience in confocal fluorescence microscopy and learned how to optimize the surrounding microenvironment to support the formation and maintenance of 3D skeletal muscle tissue.

Project status Ongoing research

Project 04 · Complementary Model

Establishment of Diverse Organoid Models

I contributed to the development of diverse organoid models and gained hands-on experience culturing human embryonic stem cells (H9), iPSCs, and patient-derived stem cells. This work included differentiating H9 cells into brain organoids and developing practical expertise in 3D tissue organization, organoid cryosectioning, whole-organoid immunostaining, and organoid-based experimental systems.

Project status Ongoing research

02 · Kyung Hee University

Human Responses to Exercise and Environmental Stress

At the Environmental Exercise Physiology Lab, I led and contributed to controlled human studies examining cardiovascular, respiratory, thermoregulatory, and perceptual responses to physiological stress. This work resulted in six journal articles, including first- and co-first-author papers.

Study 01

Ischemic Preconditioning and Ambulatory Blood Pressure

I participated in this study as a co-first author. We investigated whether ischemic preconditioning before a single session of aerobic exercise could enhance and prolong post-exercise blood pressure reduction in men with prehypertension or stage 1 hypertension. Ischemic preconditioning preserved baroreflex sensitivity for up to 24 hours and produced a greater reduction in systolic ambulatory blood pressure during sleep than aerobic exercise alone.

Frontiers in Physiology · 2024 · Co-first author

Impact of ischemic preconditioning combined with aerobic exercise on 24-h ambulatory blood pressure in men with prehypertension and stage 1 hypertension

DOI 10.3389/fphys.2024.1495648

Study 02

Exercise and Seasonal Cold Adaptation

I led this study as the first author. We examined whether regular outdoor exercise during repeated winter cold exposure influences seasonal thermoregulatory adaptation. Both cold-weather athletes and non-athletes developed cold habituation and enhanced cold-induced vasodilation after winter. Exercise did not prevent overall cold adaptation, and the improved vascular response did not translate into better manual dexterity after cold-water immersion.

Exercise Science · 2024 · First author

Exercise Does Not Prevent Thermoregulatory Adaptation to Cold Stress During the Winter Season

DOI 10.15857/ksep.2024.00458

Study 03

Remote Ischemic Preconditioning and Reperfusion Injury

I contributed to this study as a co-author. We investigated whether a single bout of remote ischemic preconditioning could protect vascular and autonomic function from ischemia-reperfusion injury in obese young men. Remote ischemic preconditioning attenuated vascular dysfunction and excessive sympathetic activation, while obesity neither worsened the injury nor abolished the protective response.

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health · 2023 · Co-author

A Single Bout of Remote Ischemic Preconditioning Suppresses Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury in Asian Obese Young Men

DOI 10.3390/ijerph20053915

Study 04

Breathing Resistance During Exercise

I contributed to this study as a co-author. We tested whether the breathing resistance commonly imposed by tight-fitting masks and respirators impairs cardiopulmonary function and aerobic exercise performance. Increasing resistance progressively reduced pulmonary ventilation, intensified exercise-induced hypoxemia and breathing discomfort, and impaired performance during graded exercise.

International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health · 2023 · Co-author

Added Breathing Resistance during Exercise Impairs Pulmonary Ventilation and Exaggerates Exercise-Induced Hypoxemia Leading to Impaired Aerobic Exercise Performance

DOI 10.3390/ijerph20105757

Study 05

Thermal Sensitivity at Rest and During Exercise

I led this study as the first author. We investigated how local heat and cold exposure, exercise, and body region influence cutaneous thermal sensitivity and the inter-threshold zone. The forehead was more sensitive to warmth and cold than the hand, while exercise and local thermal stress widened the inter-threshold zone and reduced thermal sensitivity.

Exercise Science · 2021 · First author

Effects of Local Cold and Heat Stimuli on Cutaneous Thermal Sensitivity and Inter-Threshold Zone at Rest and During Exercise

DOI 10.15857/ksep.2021.00423

Study 06

Respirator Fit and Wearing Practice

I participated in this study as a co-first author. We evaluated how KF94 mask shape and wearing method affect facial fit and inward leakage, and compared KF94 performance with surgical N95 respirators. Flat-fold KF94 masks performed better than vertical-fold designs, a headband clip improved fit over ear loops, and surgical N95 respirators achieved substantially higher fit factors despite similar filter performance.

The Korean Society of Living Environmental System · 2021 · Co-first author

Assessment of Fit Factors between Different Shapes and Wearing Practices of Filtering Facepiece Respirators

DOI 10.21086/ksles.2021.10.28.5.458
