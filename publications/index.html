Working manuscript Co-first author 2026

An integrated multi-omics and sensor framework identifies early detectable circulating biomarkers in cancer cachexia

Integrates skeletal muscle bulk RNA-seq, single-nucleus RNA-seq, and LC-MS/MS proteomics to identify Serpina3n/SERPINA3 and Ctsl/CTSL as circulating, muscle-associated biomarker candidates. The work links public dataset re-analysis, cell-origin resolution, in vivo cachexia validation, serum proteomics, and sensitive biosensor detection.
